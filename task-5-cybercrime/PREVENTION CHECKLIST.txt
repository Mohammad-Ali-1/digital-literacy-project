Stay Safe Online: A Cyber Hygiene Checklist for College Students


Financial & UPI Safety:-

1)Never Enter Your UPI PIN to Receive Money: A prevalent scam involves someone calling to say they accidentally transferred funds to you, or that you have 

won 

a prize. They will then send a "Collect Request" on apps like GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm. Remember: your UPI PIN is only required when sending money, never for 

receiving it.

2)Set Daily Transaction Limits: Open your banking app and set strict daily limits for your UPI and debit card transactions. If your phone is lost or your 

account is compromised, this minimizes the financial damage scammers can inflict before you have time to block the account.


Social Media & Communication:-

3)Verify Urgent "Help" Messages: If a friend reaches out via WhatsApp or Instagram urgently asking for a quick loan due to a sudden "emergency," call their 

phone number directly to verify. Account takeovers are common, and scammers frequently use hacked accounts to exploit a victim's contacts.

4)Lock Down Your Digital Footprint: Set your social media profiles to private. Scammers often download public photos and personal details to create fake, 

cloned profiles for identity theft or extortion (such as threatening to distribute morphed images).

5)Beware of "Easy Part-Time Job" Traps: Be highly skeptical of unsolicited WhatsApp or Telegram messages offering lucrative part-time work for simple tasks 

like "liking YouTube videos" or leaving reviews. These are almost always advance-fee frauds that eventually demand you "invest" money to unlock your 

supposed earnings.


Browsing & Account Security:-

6)Scrutinize "Free Study Material" Links: Phishing attacks are often disguised as free PDFs, exam question banks, or premium software downloads shared in 

large college WhatsApp or Telegram groups. Avoid clicking unverified links, as they can deploy malware or steal your login credentials.

7)Avoid Public Wi-Fi for Sensitive Tasks: Campus cafes and public transit networks often have unsecured Wi-Fi. Never log into your bank, UPI apps, or 

university portals on these networks unless you are using a trusted Virtual Private Network (VPN).

8)Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Everywhere: Turn on 2FA for your primary email, social media, and student accounts. Even if a scammer manages to 

steal your password, they will not be able to access your account without the secondary code sent to your phone or an authenticator app.
